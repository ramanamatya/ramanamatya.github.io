Loadings, penalties & overtime

Weekend and public holiday penalties replace the evening shift loading (the higher rate applies — they don’t stack). Each penalty is the loading on top of base; base for every worked hour is counted in Ordinary Time. Overtime is generated automatically: paid leave then worked hours fill the ordinary-hours cap below in date order, and any worked hours past the cap become overtime (first 2 hrs at T1.5, the rest at T2.0). Weekend hours that fall in the overtime zone get no weekend loading. The roster’s per-day Overtime Hrs add on top as explicit extra overtime.
